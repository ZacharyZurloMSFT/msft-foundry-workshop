Contoso Industries — Employee Handbook (Effective January 1, 2026)

SECTION 1: TIME OFF AND LEAVE

Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Full-time employees receive 15 vacation days per year, accrued monthly (1.25 days/month).
- After 3 years of service: 20 vacation days. After 7 years: 25 vacation days.
- Unused vacation days carry over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year.
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for 3+ consecutive days.

Sick Leave
- 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over.
- A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

Holidays
- 11 paid company holidays per year including New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (2 days), Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Eve.

Parental Leave
- 16 weeks paid leave for birth parents, 8 weeks for non-birth parents.
- Must have been employed for at least 12 months.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY

- All employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday in-office required for most teams).
- Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and must be documented in HR.
- Remote employees must maintain a dedicated workspace with reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps).
- Equipment stipend: $500 one-time for home office setup, plus $50/month for internet reimbursement.
- Remote employees in different time zones must maintain at least 4 hours of overlap with their team's core hours (10 AM - 3 PM ET).

SECTION 3: EXPENSE REPORTING

- All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days via the Concur expense system.
- Meals during business travel: up to $75/day.
- Hotel accommodations: up to $250/night in standard markets, $350/night in high-cost cities (NYC, SF, LA, Boston, DC).
- Flights: economy class for domestic, premium economy for international flights over 6 hours.
- Manager approval required for expenses over $500. VP approval for expenses over $2,000.
- Receipts required for all expenses over $25.

SECTION 4: BENEFITS

Health Insurance
- Company covers 85% of premiums for employees, 70% for dependents.
- Plans: PPO ($500 deductible, $3,000 max out-of-pocket) or HDHP with HSA ($1,500 deductible, company contributes $750/year to HSA).
- Open enrollment: November 1-15 each year.

Dental and Vision
- Dental: 100% preventive, 80% basic procedures, 50% major procedures. Annual maximum: $2,000.
- Vision: Annual exam covered, $200 allowance for frames/lenses every 2 years.

Retirement
- 401(k) with company match: 100% match on first 4% of salary, 50% match on next 2%.
- Employees are immediately vested in their own contributions. Company match vests over 3 years (33% per year).
- Eligible to participate after 90 days of employment.

Life Insurance
- Company-paid life insurance: 2x annual salary up to $500,000.
- Optional supplemental life insurance available at group rates.

Professional Development
- $2,500 annual learning budget per employee for courses, conferences, books.
- 3 paid learning days per year (separate from PTO).

SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT

- Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to your manager and HR within 30 days.
- Company property (laptops, software, data) must be used for business purposes only.
- Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- The Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-ETHX) is available 24/7 for anonymous reporting.
